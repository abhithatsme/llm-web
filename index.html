How Text Becomes Intelligence: The 3-Step LLM Pipeline

This interactive laboratory demonstrates how Large Language Models turn human words into numbers (tokens), place them into 3D geometric space (embeddings), and predict what comes next.

Step 1: Tokenizer & Subwords → Step 2: 3D Vector Embeddings → Step 3: Pipeline Tracer & Next-Word Prediction

1. Tokenizer & Subword Vocabulary

Computers do not read letters or words directly. They break text into numeric tokens. Watch how unknown words are split into subwords.

Words kept in vocabulary: Live Subword Splitter

Drag left to shrink the vocabulary. Dropped words get split into smaller subword pieces (##suffix) — watch the token count increase live!

2. Embeddings in 3D Space

Sentences are converted into 64-dimensional vectors and projected down to 3D with Principal Component Analysis (PCA). Similar ideas cluster together.

Tip: Click and drag to rotate the 3D scene. Scroll to zoom in/out.

3. Try Your Own Word & Prediction Tracer

Follow your text completely through the pipeline: from tokens, to 3D point, to closest sentences, to next-token prediction.
